SW1 - Whirly Tube

Demonstrating light and colour.

A fun musical instrument that produces harmonic musical tones when swung in a circle. When swung at different speeds produces discreet notes/modes of vibration. Can be used to explore resonance and the physics of sound, as well as Bernoulli's principle
