S11 - Interference/Beats

Demonstrate constructive and destructive interference in sound waves.

Using a setup with and amplifier, and two signal generators you can generate sounds of specific frequencies with a two speaker setup - each speaker is tuned by it's own function generator. By tuning the speakers to the same frequency, you'll hear constructive interference. Connecting the speakers with opposing polarity and the same frequency demonstrates destructive interference. By setting the speakers to slightly different frequencies, you'll hear a periodic pulsing known as 'beats'.
