L40 - Ripple Tank

Glass-bottomed tray of water used in physics to study the behavior and properties of two-dimensional water waves.

A lamp shines light from above (or below) through the transparent tank. The water's surface acts like a series of lenses, projecting moving shadows of the wave peaks and troughs onto a white sheet of paper or screen below. A motorized paddle or dipper vibrates at the surface to create regular waves, such as straight plane waves or circular waves. A stroboscope (or strobed light) can sync with the wave frequency to make the fast-moving ripples appear slow or stationary for easier measurement.
