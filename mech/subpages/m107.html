M107 - Bicycle Gyroscope

Wheel gyroscope demonstration.

This is a bicycle wheel gyroscope setup, a fascinating physics experiment used to demonstrate angular momentum, gravitational torque, and gyroscopic precession. If you hang the bicycle wheel by the rope from one end of its axle without spinning it and let go, the wheel simply flops down vertically. Gravity pulls down on teh wheel's center of mass, creating a downward force and a torque that tips the axle over. If you spin the wheel rapidly and then suspend it by the rope from only one side, it seemingly defies gravity. Instead of falling down, the axle remains horizontal and the entire wheel slowly rotates horizontally around the rope.
