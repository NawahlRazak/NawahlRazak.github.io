M167 - Oscillators Coupled by string

Demonstration of coupled oscillators.

A coupled pendulum apparatus set up on a stand with hanging weights of varying lengths and positions connected by a string. When one pendulum is set into motion, its energy transfers step-by-step through the connecting string to the neighboring pendulums. This setup demonstrates how mechanical waves and energy move through a coupled oscillator system without transporting mass. Pendulums of matching lengths respond most strongly due to resonance and identical natural frequencies.
