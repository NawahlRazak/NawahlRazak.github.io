M95 - Loop the Loop

gravity provides centripetal force - a demonstration on conservation of energy.

At the very top of the loop, the ball needs enough speed (velocity v) so that gravity provides the necessary centripetal force to keep it on the track (v² ≥ gR). If you release a ball from a height equal to the top of the loop (2R), it fails to make it around and falls off because it lacks speed at the top. Because a solid ball rolls without slipping, it also gains rotational kinetic energy alongside translational speed and gravitational potential energy. For a solid sphere on a circular track, the minimum release height required to maintain contact all the way around is about 2.7R (or 2.7 times the radius of the loop measured from the bottom).
