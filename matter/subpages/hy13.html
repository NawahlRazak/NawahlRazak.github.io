HY13 - Vortex Cannon

Demonstrates the action of a vortex using smoke rings

A large drum is set up with a thick plastic membrane on one end and a hole on the other end. Drop a smoke bomb or a liter of liquid nitrogen down a small hole through the drum cylinder, and after about 5 seconds, tap lightly on the membrane. Smoke rings will pop out on the other end. Works well with a strobe light highlighting the rings. Request well in advance so that liquid nitrogen can be arranged.
