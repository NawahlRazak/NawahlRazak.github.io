AP6 - Bernoulli Blower

Demonstration that uses a fast-moving stream of air to suspend an object.

A strong upward airflow comes from the blower. This creates a zone of lower pressure rught around the floating object. The slower, stiller air surrounding the stream has a higher pressure. If the ball tries to drift away, the higher outside pressure pushes it right back into the center of the air stream. Gravity and the upward force of the air balance out, making the ball hover.
