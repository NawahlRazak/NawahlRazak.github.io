EM134 - Mutual Induction Coils

Experiments include a magnetic field of one coil inducing a voltage in the other coil, the effect of iron in a magnetic circuit, the basic transformer theory, making electromagnets and more.

The apparatus consists of a small coil, a larger coil that fits inside the larger coil, and an iron core that fits inside the small coil to enhance the induced magnetic field. The apparaturs can be connected to show the principle of electromagnetic induction, particularly the principle of a step up and step down transformer.
