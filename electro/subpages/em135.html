EM134 - Induction Coils

Experiments include a magnetic field of one coil inducing a voltage in the other coil,the basic transformer theory, making electromagnets, LC LR and LRC circuits.

These can be used on their own or with other components to demonstrate Induction, Mutual induction, LC circuits, LRC circuits etc.
