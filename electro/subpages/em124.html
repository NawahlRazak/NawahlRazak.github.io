EM124 - Electron Beam in Uniform Magnetic Field

Investigation of the deflection of an electron beam in a uniform magnetic field.

This complete experimental system is used to determine an electron's specific charge and investigate the deflection of electron beams in a uniform magnetic field... The system comes complete with: a fine-beam tube, a pair of Helmholtz coils for generating a uniform magnetic field, an operating unit for power supply. The fine beam tube and the pair of Helmholtz coils are mounted on the operating unit, with the fine beam tube fully rotatable about its vertical axis. The tube and both coils are internally connected to the operating unit, eliminating the need for any external wiring. All supply voltages for the tube, as well as the current through the Helmholtz coils, are fully adjustable. The anode voltage and coil current are displayed digitally and can also be accessed as equivalent voltage values.
